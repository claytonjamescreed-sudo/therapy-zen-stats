# Align Pepper with HPC Billing’s brand

## What will change
- Use HPC Billing’s official Healthcare Partners logo in the signed-in header and sign-in page, while keeping **Pepper** as the product name.
- Rework the app palette to match the live HPC Billing site: deep navy, bright blue, white, soft blue-gray, and green status accents.
- Update visible ownership language from Pepper to HPC Billing where it refers to the team managing accounts or onboarding.
- Replace the default favicon with an icon derived from the HPC Billing brand.
- Update page titles and descriptions so Pepper is presented as an HPC Billing product.

## What stays the same
- All dashboard features, permissions, demo data, and navigation behavior.
- The Pepper product name and agent name.
- No white-label or resale features.

## Validation
- Check the sign-in and dashboard at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Confirm the logo, navigation, buttons, statuses, favicon, and page titles render correctly without overlaps.
